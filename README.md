# Valeno Legal Website

Official legal and compliance website for **ShaktiSetu — by Valeno**.

This website provides users with important information regarding privacy, terms of use, and account/data deletion.

## Pages

- `/` — Valeno / ShaktiSetu legal portal
- `/privacy` — Privacy Policy
- `/terms` — Terms & Conditions
- `/delete-account` — Account & Data Deletion

## About

**Valeno** is the parent brand behind ShaktiSetu, a women-safety technology product designed to provide safety-focused features and emergency assistance tools.
